# Gazdura

An independent product I'm building to help people understand airline points, status and perks inside the AI assistant they already use. It starts with Alaska Atmos Rewards and is in early, founder-led testing.

[Project website](https://gazdura.com)

## The problem

I used to shop for flights mainly by price and convenience. Learning how status, points, perks and alliances fit together changed how I thought about a trip: not just today's fare, but what a choice could mean for future travel.

The rules are scattered and conditional. Fare, timing, eligibility and who is traveling can all change the answer. I wanted to make those rules easier to understand without expecting every traveler to become a program expert.

## What I chose to build

- **Advice inside an existing assistant.** A connector supplies reviewed knowledge to Claude or ChatGPT rather than asking people to learn another travel app.
- **Understanding before transactions.** The product explains rules. It does not book travel or access a traveler's airline account, balance or live seats.
- **Reviewed knowledge with visible limits.** Rules carry the date they were checked. Gazdura tells the assistant what isn't covered yet; that does not guarantee how the assistant will present the answer.
- **A deliberately narrow starting point.** Alaska Atmos Rewards first, with partial coverage of earning, checked bags, upgrades, companion fares, airline credit cards and status on alliance partners. Companion fares and credit cards have thinner coverage.
- **No airline credentials.** The guest connector does not require a Gazdura account or an airline login.

## What I'm learning

Testing so far has been founder-led, including agent-assisted checks. It is not evidence of adoption or broad customer validation. Early chats exposed both an incorrect answer and an assistant that did not use the connector at all. That makes the whole experience the test: not just whether the reference material is correct, but whether the assistant uses it and communicates its limits.

## Where it could go

My working hypothesis is that clearer loyalty guidance can help travelers judge the longer-term value of an airline ecosystem, not only the cheapest fare. That is a hypothesis to test, not a demonstrated conversion or retention result.

Other major airlines and AI assistants are directions for expansion, not current coverage. I see this work as a way to demonstrate what an advisory layer could offer travelers and the businesses serving them.

Gazdura is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by an airline or loyalty program. Coverage is partial and rules change. Confirm with the airline before booking.
